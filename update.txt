Gameplay
* Green sector reserves: green infantry will respawn until sector reserve pool (2x original infantry garrison) is depleted.
* Green sector stronghold: most green sectors will spawn strongholds, which will instantly deplete reserves when destroyed.
* Order combat air patrol: calls in temporary A2A over selected airfield sector or forward airbases.
* Secure aircraft wreck: securing manned aircraft wrecks rewards players with 1/3 the cost of the aircraft.
* Designate team priority: allows squad leaders to designate a sector, fob, stronghold, or fast travel asset as a team priority.
* Travel to frontline: FT to team priority, intended to help get new players into the fight.
* Conscription: requests team to get to team priority.
* Fast travel frontline (for sector): button on map picks closest FT point (tent, vehicle, or SL/squad) to sector stronghold or center (if no stronghold present).
* Fast travel home and respawn now puts player next to flag.
* Upgrade FOB: can now upgrade FOB to forward airbase with high supply cost, allows deployment of planes.
* Secure player: securing downed player destroys their tent if within 100m, force respawn destroys nearby tent automatically.
* Mine clear: Bobcat, Utility Marid can now clear mines with a mine clearing charge.
* Revamped customization system to be more closely tied to Arsenal (now free). Loadouts available in gear menu.
* Supply boat is now a conversion rather than deployment.
* FOB supply crates model switched.
* Removed winter holiday events.
* Deployable items now show display name in action menu.
* Moved demolition action down priority list.
* Removed fast travel contested.
* Firing laser munition in loal mode reveals launcher to enemy for 120s, no longer dazzleable.
* Combined attach/detach for both flatbed and helo slingloader, now shows closest vehicle.
* All area scanners (sector scan, fob, stronghold, recon optics) will now detect enemy mines.
* Added smart mines to green sector spawn pool.
* Removed anti-heavy vehicle checks on carrier.
* Enabled deployment of sandbags above ground level.
* Option in settings to not spawn with UAV terminal.
* Bulk delete: option in strategy to temporarily disable asset removal confirmation.
* Bobcat can now drag cargo behind it, recommend not to use on anything other than aircraft wrecks.
* Slammer integral mortar now shows ammunition status.
* Polls now immediately update players without respawning.
* Progressively show more map links as hover time increases.
* Having a Bobcat/Marid utility nearby increases demolition speed by 20%.
* Reward points for revealing sector.
* Unloading now loops until unloading is complete or cancelled.
* RTB failure now shows why jet can't RTB.
* Consolidated Blackfish logistics system under flatbed type.
* Gear and vehicle ammo crates are despawned after 5 minutes.
* Show kill asset in feed.
* Added translations for more menu items.
* Disabled removing stronghold from map if sector is target selected by enemy team.
