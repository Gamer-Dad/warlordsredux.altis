Gameplay
* New spawn menu: menu now combines vehicles, squads, and spawn point.
* Sector reinforcements: player owned sectors now have a limited reinforcement count, depleted by killing defenders.
* Once depleted of sector reinforcements, these sector features are disabled: conscription, vehicle paradrop, asset purchase, sector scan.
* Sectors with remaining reinforcements give +4x capture modifiers.
* Supplies (formerly FOB supplies) can be added to sectors to increase reinforcement count.
* Supplies can now only be purchased at non-FOB helipads.
* Supplies now convert to fortified rally points instead of supply boats.
* Lowered forward base upgrade cost 20,000->10,000 supplies.
* Removed sector fortification mechanic. All captured sectors self-fortify after 5 minutes.
* Added auto-SAM option that allows players to control SAM open-fire range.
* Added button to call in combat air patrol on nearest eligible target in Air Defense category.
* Added communication menu options.
* Added confirmation message when saving to non-empty loadout slot or clearing.
* Added new cables to carriers.
* Added option to skip conscription if player is in priority sector.
* Added option to turn auto-vector off by default.
* AI ordered into player's vehicles will be force moved into its seat, removed FT AI to vehicle button.
* Anti-air missiles now use consistent damage model, independent of lag/network latency.
* Area capture modifier now only applies in PvP sectors, not against green sectors.
* Combat air patrol: upgraded munition BIM9x->AMRAAM-D, radius decreased 7->6km, cooldown decreased 30->20 minutes.
* Combat air patrol home: a mini version of combat air patrol can now be called in home base with faster cooldown and smaller area.
* Demolishables are no longer claimable.
* Disabled repairs above 100m altitude.
* Electronic warfare strength is now increased/decreased by securing enemy air drones.
* ELO/ratings no longer affected by match duration.
* Enemies nearby purchase lock radius increased 50->125m.
* Fast travel now generates audio cue.
* Field repairs now repair turret and cannon.
* FOB owners can now call for paradrops.
* Heavy+ APS: no longer stops SDBs when dropped in manual mode, stops 1 SDB when dropped in GPS guided mode.
* Hold freelook over map to see detailed mode.
* Increased deployment cancel distance for installables and carriers from 30->50m.
* Increased green sector vehicle difficulty.
* Increased ground launched R77/AMRAAM/Zephyr notch immunity range 1->2km, reduced projectile speed 1.1->0.8x.
* Increased spectrum jammer range, uses much smaller interface, no longer requires hold click.
* Increased tolerance of mine charge, will clear minefields as long as the cable is touching.
* Laser guided munitions range increased, targets now shown on pilot HUD.
* Lock stronghold doors against hostile players.
* Map now includes rearm, refuel, and repair actions for vehicles and AI control on/off for drones.
* Max mines increased 30->100.
* Maximum autonomous assets 4->3.
* Maximum capture time decreased 150->120s, fewer AT minefields.
* Minefield victims now hear VO.
* Mines and explosives placed by player are now reclaimed by player on respawn.
* Mine clear charges now delete destructive explosives rather than trigger them.
* Moved remote designator to remote control category.
* Paradropping vehicles now reward pilots with support points.
* Paradrops can now be refreshed with rearms.
* Paradrop calls over water are no longer allowed. Altitude minimum raised 100->200m.
* Players can initiate paradrops on paradrop-capable aircraft from the map.
* Players no longer spawn with watches.
* Raised ELO balance algorithm minimum ELO: 1,300->1,500.
* Raised maximum bank balance $50,000->100,000.
* Recon vehicles now earn spotting points for owners even if player is not in driver seat.
* Reduced chance of explosives teamkills.
* Reduced time to revive/secure players.
* Removed death info screen.
* Removed restrictions around deployment height.
* Renamed combat air patrol to no-fly zone.
* Renamed Sidearm missiles for Opfor to Kh-31P ARM.
* Repair, resupply now reward points to the vehicle owner.
* RTB now prevented only by jets exceeding $10,000 in value.
* Scale respawn times for territory control in early game and for losing teams (min: 20s).
* Securing player no longer auto-deletes tent.
* Securing valuable enemy air wrecks now reveals flying enemy air assets on map.
* Sector auto-spawn now includes squadmates and tents.
* Sector income decreased $12->8/km^2/minute.
* Sector scan: re-enabled on map (not in strategy menu), initial delay 10->20s.
* Sectors connected to home base get a +1x capture bonus.
* Slingloading ropes can now be significantly shortened with action.
* Slingloading/deployables no longer force out/lock crews.
* Sound effect now plays for proximity hits.
* Spartan/SAAMI flight speed doubled, notch immunity range 2,500->3,000m.
* Squad size max is now 9->10.
* Squads can now be locked and disbanded. Open squads can be joined by anyone.
* Squads can now spawn on all players in squad.
* Surrender disabled.
* Team rally points are no longer converted from squad leader tents, now deployable from light helos, harder to damage.
* Tents are much more easily destroyed by any damage, but can be repeatedly respawned onto.
* Transport rewards are no longer doubled if sector is defensive, scale by type.
* USS Phalanx and Archer are now indestructible.
* Vehicle reset is now free, and flipped vehicles will get an option to reset.
* VLS destroyers disabled.
* When max AI count is 1, remove AI timer mechanic.
* Wipe loadout is now delete loadout, which deletes the last selected loadout for a vehicle/aircraft.
