Warlords Redux 2.8.0 Patch 7

Assets
* New: Shahed/LUCAS Drones.
* Removed: Scout Drones
* Strike Wasp: increased ammo for incendiary munitions.
* Marid (Utility): re-added .50 cal, cost $1,800->2,000, now immune to AT minefields.
* Bobcat: now immune to AT minefields.
* Slammer II: upgraded APS to Heavy+, which stops SDBs, cost $8,500->9,000.
* Angara-K, Futura: upgraded APS to Heavy+.
* AT Minefield: visibility distance on map increased from 400->800m.
* Large AT Minefields now come in large, circular, and hex shapes.
* Ghost Hawk: added fast travel option.
* Ammo/Repair/Fuel trucks: added item deployment option.
* Heavy Recon: replaced IR tent deployment with fighting position.

Gameplay
* Added Charkia Mine as a sector.
* Added option to adjust incendiary effect for better performance (does not affect damage).
* Added queueing system for timed prompt (squad invite, remote munitions, conscription, FF forgive).
* Removed AI get in, replaced with ability to fast travel AI directly into currently occupied vehicle from map.
* Reduced balancer count limit: 5->3.
* Increased incendiary kill radius greatly, reduced airburst height.
* Show team average player rating on scoreboard.
* Added deployable decoy for Centurion.
* Added restock action, grenade turret to fighting positions.
* Show team priority as circle on map.
* Reduced FOB upgrade cost $25,000->20,000.

Bugfixes
* Fixed light unguided rockets penetrating light aps.
* Fixed heavier munitions not going through light aps.
* Fixed duplicate sound source for incendiary munitions.
* Fixed infantry spawning death in home.
* Fixed friendly fire auto-punishing when multiple units are team killed.
* Performance pass on asset map button calculations.
* Fixed not being able to FT AI to your location if AI is downed or in water.
* Fixed AI personnel counting into asset limit.
* Fixed Mohawk universal slingload system not working with some large vehicles.
* Fixed green air wrecks disappearing when green sector is captured.
* Performance pass on teamkill forgiveness.
* Fixed some cases of green jets crashing on spawn.
* Fixed script error for location scanner.
* Performance pass on team priority, map buttons.
* Reworked map selection screen.
* Fixed exploit where players can call in combat air patrol on non-helipad sectors.
* Fixed exploit where consumption mechanics can be rearmed for free.
