Munition changes
* New: AGM-122 Sidearm: anti-radiation missile on Scalpel base, consumes 13 APS charges when used in ARM role.
* New: Bunker busters: remote TV-guided, can do damage to strongholds and FOBs.
* New: LOAL mode now supported for long-range Fox-3s, can kill enemy aircaft at extreme ranges.
* Changed: short-range Fox-2 missiles have significantly increased probability of hit.
* Changed: anti-radiation missiles now attack modes based on the nose pitch of the aircraft (point down for more direct attack, up for top attack).
* Changed: HARM and KH58 are no longer dazzleable.
* Changed: when used in non-LOAL lock modes, anti-radiation missiles only deplete 1 APS.
* Changed: remote TV-guided munitions no longer require ground support station, controller must be in squad.
* Changed: switchblade speed and range doubled, can now hit low altitude air targets.
* Changed: replaced all 240 round chaffs with 300 rounds.
* Changed: attack helo device jammer now APS jammer, no longer requires player gunner.
* Changed: waypoint now visible on more remote munitions.
* Changed: Hercules approximate location will be marked on enemy map after 5 launches, with the approximation closer with every launch.
* Changed: anti-air missiles auto detonate upon going blind.

Ground vehicles
* Nyx (AT): re-added MG, reverted Firefist reload speed back to vanilla.
* Marid (ATGM): added 4 missiles to ammo.
* Zamak MRL: removed APS.
* Panther: replaced 40mm with 30mm autocannon.
* Marshall Light: replaced minigun with .50 cal.
* BTR-KM Kamysh: replaced 5-mag Titan AT with 8-mag Scalpel AT.
* Rhino MGS UP: replaced 120mm cannon with 125mm.
* Slammer II: removed recon optics, retain datalink visuals.
* Switchblade: ammo reduced from 10 to 2.
* Hercules: hard lock range reduced from 8->6.5 km.

Rotary wing
* Pawnee: added triple-rack Scalpel pylon, added Skyfire pylon, added HMD, replaced minigun with 20mm, added countermeasures. (rotary wing)
* Orca: added DAGR-M pylon, added quad-rack Sidearm pylon, added R73 pylon, added BIM-9X pylon, added KH-58 pylon. (rotary wing)
* Falcon: added ASRAAM pylon. (fixed wing)
* Hellcat: added faster and more countermeasures, added Scalpel option. (rotary wing)
* Ghost Hawk Block II: added more countermeasures. (rotary wing)
* Kajman: added R73 pylon, added 2 mags HE gunner ammo. (rotary wing)
* Blackfoot: added 4 racks of triple-rack Sidearms, added 1 mag HE gunner ammo. (rotary wing)
* Blackfoot Block II: added 4 racks of triple-rack Sidearms, added 1 mag HE gunner ammo. (rotary wing)
* For spawning purposes, non-armed Blackfish are considered helipad spawns now.

Fixed wing
* Caesar (Bomb): bombs are now GPS-guided rather than remote TV-guided. (fixed wing)
* Xian: added 4 SDBs (pilot) and replaced gun with more effective 30mm. (fixed wing)
* Wipeout: added 2 pylons of bunker buster bombs, added DAGR-M pylon. (fixed wing)
* Buzzard: can now launch remote TV-guided bombs, added one pylon of bunker buster bombs. (fixed wing)
* Neophron: added 2 pylons of bunker buster bombs. (fixed wing)
* Growler: added 4 pylons of Sidearms, added LOAL support, added 8km radius threat detector.
* Shikra: added 1 pylon of quad-rack SDBs. (fixed wing)
* Shikra (Stealth/Recon): added R77 for 2 pylons, added 2 pylons of quad-rack GPS-enabled SDBs, added Sidearm racks to 5 pylons, enabled GPS guidance on KAB-250, added LOAL support, added 16km radius threat detector. (fixed wing)
* Black Wasp (Stealth/Recon): added quad-rack GPS-enabled SDBs, enabled GPS guidance on GBUs, added Sidearm racks to 4 pylons, added LOAL support. (fixed wing)

Boat
* Attack boats: replaced 20mm with 30mm autocannons, added 2 AA missiles.

Map
* Moved Kavirida Caves to Kavirida.
* Remove multiple connections around Dorida.
* Removed connection from Quarry to Chalkeia.
* Connect Xirolimni Dam to Poliakko.
* Significantly reduced size of Sfaka.
* Significantly reduced size/income of carrier sectors.
* Added harbors to Hill 275 and Thronos Castle.
* Removed preset Strider spawns from Skopos Base.
* Removed obstructions on carrier during attack phase, moved AI spawns, added vehicle spawns.
* All non-runway sectors other than Atsalis can now be home base.
* The following sectors have a small chance of not spawning: Abdera, Aktinarki Ruins, Central Terminal, Didymos Turbines, Hill 275, Hillside Chapel, Ioannina, Koroni, Lakka, Neri, Paros, Poliakko, Skopos Castle Ruins, Theater Ruins.
* Adjusted sector vehicle spawns to depend on sector value/size, sectors spawn double vehicles.
* Sectors connected to home base may now spawn aircraft.
* Added destroyers: 30% chance to replace each carrier and a 70% chance to spawn in Pyrgos bay.

Interface
* Squad: can now FT to squadmates/squad leader via menu.
* Killfeed, kill sounds: optional and volume controllable via settings.
* Loadout menu: allows more weapons and granular ammo controls, rebalanced weapon levels.
* Badges: can earn and apply badges in place of level.
* Death info: now shows killer badge.
* Report: refreshed report interface.
* Scoreboard: now shows WL relevant info and scores.
* Targeting menus: now can be easily controllable in flight.
* Turret visualizer: added turret visualizer for aircraft that have restrictive gunner turrets.
* Vehicle menu: can repair/rearm/lock/remove vehicles remotely.
* Allow using recon optics out of gunner camera view.
* Enemy target reset is now a system message.
* Added approx funds for teammates in transfer menu tooltips.
* Scanner circles and active EW now visible to enemies on map.
* Removed radio sound when opening asset menu.
* Capture bar now thinner.
* Adjusted missile camera large mode to not steal focus.
* Removed recruit tag for more granular level tags.
* Removed attack/attack2 sector markers.
* Level 50 restriction for placing map markers.
* Control UAV actions should be more intuitive, not lose when dead.

ECM jammers
* For: scout/attack helos, Xian.
* Allows equipped aircraft to jam and destroy incoming missiles.
* Limited by ammo, recharged on a timer, more pods = more ammo, faster recharge.
* Jamming time affected by distance.

Map voting
* Reduced price of sector reset to $50.
* Removed sector reset ctrl-y/n voting.
* Sector reset will no longer unset current attack sector until new sector is selected.
* Sector reset timer is now personal to prevent griefing.
* Moved surrender voting to map vote (reduced voting power, individual).

New shortcuts
* Added more granular HMD settings, can now separately adjust view distance for different vehicle types (key: binoculars, default: B).
* APS and dazzler can now all be switched on/off (key: cycle thrown item, default: Ctrl+G).
* Added rebindable keys for earplugs (key: buldozer switch camera, default: insert) and cqb mode (key: zeus delete, default: delete).

FOB
* FOB can be repaired on map to full for 5K supplies, repairs 1 point every minute automatically.
* Added FOB min range circle on map.
* FOB uses bigger crate.
* Player who places FOB can delete it directly from the map.
* Increase FOB sector support range to 2km, decreased circle to 75m.
* If FOB placement trips "too close" restriction, allow position reselection without hold action.

Rewards
* Attacking or defending sector now gives active income: $10 per cap contribution, every 20 seconds.
* Added reward for recon scanning if player is in scanning vehicle: $20 per infantry, $100 per vehicle.
* Triggering dazzler now gives reward: $10 per charge depleted.
* APS rewards now vary based on consumption: $50 per charge depleted.
* Added reward for disabling enemy vehicles: $50 per immobilization.

Infantry
* Flag now allows loadout reload.
* Added bipods to gear crate / attachment options.
* Revive now defaults to staying in downed state until respawn, can use escape respawn menu but will not speed up respawn.
* Thrown chemlights now deploy tents instead of backpacks.
* AI may be revived until you die.
* Enemies not on carrier deck will no longer restrict carrier deck asset spawning.
* Respawning into base under attack spawns defender reinforcements in parachutes.
* Removed range limit for fast travel tag-along AI.
* Increased enemies nearby restriction radius to 150m, added option to Fast Travel Home not subject to restriction.
* Removed last/saved loadout options.
* Infantry no longer spawn with UAV terminal by default, can use vehicle manager to connect to UAVs instead.

Demolition
* Demolition now requires continuous action and occurs in steps.
* Damaged strongholds can be repaired on map for money.
* Damaged FOBs can be repaired on map for money and 5000 FOB supplies.
* Friendly demolition is now disabled by default, must be enabled in settings (punishable).

Misc
* Added occasional night, earthquakes.
* Reworked repair tires/tracks to be locked action.
* Reworked revive to be locked action.
* Separated buildables (max: 25) vs assets (max: 10) for the purpose of asset limits.
* Increased "AI get in" range to 150m.
* Aircraft flying off map gets Return to Base scroll wheel option (cost $1,000), if not damaged too recently.
* Raised vehicle paradrop height to 150m, limit now per-vehicle, can no longer paradrop to sector targeted by enemy team.
* Removed Pelican bomb remote control arming mechanic; it is now automatically armed and auto-detonates upon destruction.
* UAVs spawn as personal assets.
