Warlords Redux 2.8.0 Patch 6

Assets
* Removed: Gryphon Interceptor
* Removed: Growler
* Added: Strike Wasp
* Added: Gorgon (Mortar)

Gameplay
* All players can now call conscription.
* Conscription cost: $3,000->500.
* Added incendiary munitions.
* Demolition charges now do damage to demolishables, limited to 5 deployed at once.
* Mora: internalized mortar.
* Hunter Apex: cap power 2->4, aps medium->dazzler.
* Hellcat: removed internal 20mm cannon.
* Huron/Mohawk: unified price to $700.
* Changed EW minigame.
* Rearm APS: vehicles with heavy APS can now rearm their APS in the field, from outside.
* Internal vehicle mortars now fire incendiary.
* Enemy nearby distance lock: 75->50m.
* Moved remote munitions, ask forgiveness, and squad invites to soft-prompt system.
* Blackfoot cost reduction by 2k, increased AMRAAM slots.
* Unified dazzler and aps. Dazzlers now have deadzone and angle check and similar rewards.
* New imbalance algorithm.
* Disabled helipad home sectors.
* Large water-majority regions: 50->60% area calc.
* Zaros: reduced height.

Bugfixes
* Fixed bug where vehicles could be ordered but not placed.
* Fixed vehicle mortar to have more understandable name.
* Fixed combat air warning not being visible enough. Now has audio and more prominent visual cues.
* Fixed combat air caller name not being shown to enemies.
* Fixed smart mines beeping not deleting after smart mines are killed, removed audio entirely.
* Fixed spectrum interface showing dead UAVs.
* Fixed dead players near team priority not being shown conscription notice.
* Fixed certain cases where UI elements would visually block conscription notices.
* Fixed bug where certain UAVs would show up on enemy datalink regardless of EW status.
* Fixed AFK player count affecting AI limits.
* Fixed some AA missiles getting deleted for friendly fire check.
* Fixed strongholds disappearing from map.
* Fixed travel frontline not considering defensive sectors.
* Fixed anti-air missile detonation message duplicates.
* Performance pass on APS, strongholds.
* Disabled spawn empty setting for potential BE kicks.
