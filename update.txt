Munition changes
* New: AGM-122 Sidearm: anti-radiation missile on Scalpel base, consumes 13 APS charges (only when used in ARM role), can be manually/IR guided as regular Scalpels.
* New: Bunker busters: remote TV-guided, can do damage to strongholds and FOBs.
* New: LOAL mode now supported for long-range Fox-3s, can kill enemy aircaft at extreme ranges.
* Changed: short-range Fox-2 missiles have significantly increased probability of hit.
* Changed: anti-radiation missiles now attack modes based on the nose pitch of the aircraft (point down for more direct attack, up for top attack).
* Changed: remote TV-guided munitions no longer require ground support station, controller must be in squad.
* Changed: switchblade speed and range doubled, can now hit low altitude air targets.

Asset-specific changes
* Marid (ATGM): added 4 missiles to ammo.
* Zamak MRL: removed APS.
* Panther: replaced 40mm with 30mm autocannon.
* Marshall Light: replaced minigun with .50 cal.
* BTR-KM Kamysh: replaced 5-mag Titan AT with 8-mag Scalpel AT.
* Rhino MGS UP: replaced 120mm cannon with 125mm.
* Slammer II: removed recon optics, retain datalink visuals.
* Switchblade: ammo reduced from 10 to 2.
* Hercules: hard lock range reduced from 8->6.5 km.
* Caesar (Bomb): bombs are now GPS-guided rather than remote TV-guided.
* Pawnee: added triple-rack Scalpel pylon, added Skyfire pylon, added HMD, replaced minigun with 20mm, added countermeasures.
* Orca: added DAGR-M pylon.
* Falcon: added ASRAAM pylon.
* Kajman: added R73 pylon.
* Blackfoot: added 4 racks of triple-rack Sidearms.
* Blackfoot Block II: added 4 racks of triple-rack Sidearms.
* Xian: added 4 SDBs (pilot) and replaced gun with more effective 30mm.
* Wipeout: added 2 pylons of bunker buster bombs, added DAGR-M pylon.
* Buzzard: can now launch remote TV-guided bombs, added one pylon of bunker buster bombs.
* Neophron: added 2 pylons of bunker buster bombs.
* Growler: added 4 pylons of Sidearms, added LOAL support, added 8km radius threat detector.
* Shikra: added 1 pylon of quad-rack SDBs.
* Shikra (Stealth/Recon): added R77 for 2 pylons, added 2 pylons of quad-rack GPS-enabled SDBs, added Sidearm racks to 5 pylons, enabled GPS guidance on KAB-250, added LOAL support, added 16km radius threat detector.
* Black Wasp (Stealth/Recon): added quad-rack GPS-enabled SDBs, enabled GPS guidance on GBUs, added Sidearm racks to 4 pylons, added LOAL support.
* Attack boats: replaced 20mm with 30mm autocannons, added 2 AA missiles.

New interfaces
* Squad: can now FT to squadmates/squad leader via menu.
* Killfeed, kill sounds: optional and volume controllable via settings.
* Loadout menu: allows more weapons and granular ammo controls, rebalanced weapon levels.
* Badges: can earn and apply badges in place of level.
* Death info: now shows killer badge.
* Scoreboard: now shows WL relevant info and scores.
* Targeting menus: now can be easily controllable in flight.
* Turret visualizer: added turret visualizer for aircraft that have restrictive gunner turrets.
* Vehicle menu: can repair/rearm/lock/remove vehicles remotely.

ECM jammers 
* For: scout/attack helos, Xian.
* Allows equipped aircraft to jam and destroy incoming missiles.
* Limited by ammo, recharged on a timer, more pods = more ammo, faster recharge.
* Jamming time affected by distance.

Map voting

* Sector reset will no longer unset current attack sector until new sector is selected.
